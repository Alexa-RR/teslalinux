About

Teslalinux
Android apps in your Tesla's browser, served from a Raspberry Pi with an LTE modem.
Teslalinux is a fork of Tesla Android (github.com/tesla-android). Tesla Android runs full Android on the Pi; Teslalinux runs Linux and runs each app under the Android Translation Layer. This page, its display, touch, GPS and audio protocols come from Tesla Android.
